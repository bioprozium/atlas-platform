# Asset licensing

The repository's [MIT License](../LICENSE) covers project source code. Models, textures, photographs, scans, datasets, annotations, and other atlas content are distributed under the terms recorded for each asset. A single atlas bundle may contain assets with different terms.

## Before contributing an asset

- Verify who owns the asset and record where it came from. Public availability alone does not grant redistribution rights.
- Check that the terms permit the intended use, including any adaptations, web and app distribution, and offline downloads. Check each dependency separately, including textures, reference imagery, and source datasets.
- Keep assets with missing or incompatible terms out of the repository and release bundles until the necessary rights are documented. For third-party materials, retain required notices and follow attribution, share-alike, and other applicable terms.

## Records and distribution

Keep a licensing and provenance record for each asset or collection whose contents share the same terms. Include the title or identifier, version, creator and rights holder, original source, license name and version or permission reference, required attribution, and a description of modifications. Use an SPDX identifier where one exists and link to the applicable terms.

Carry these records into published asset packs and keep attribution and license notices available to users offline. Preserve the upstream notices when converting, simplifying, or combining assets.
